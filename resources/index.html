Property

Commercial & personal property risk checker.

Enter a ZIP or street address. We geocode it, pull active National Weather Service alerts, and look up USGS earthquake history within 100 miles.

NWS + USGS. Flood-zone maps from FEMA are a separate product.

Auto & fleet

VIN decoder for personal auto and commercial fleet.

Paste a 17-character VIN. NHTSA VPIC returns year, make, model, body class, vehicle type, drive type, fuel, and displacement — then we route you to the right quote form.

Decodes as you finish the 17th character, or on submit.

Life & annuities

Inflation and cash erosion calculator.

Uses BLS CPI-U (CUUR0000SA0) for a trailing 12-month inflation rate, then shows what uninvested cash is worth in today’s dollars versus hypothetical fixed and indexed growth. Illustrations only.
